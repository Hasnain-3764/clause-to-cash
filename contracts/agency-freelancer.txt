4. COPYWRITER FEES
4.1 The Copywriter shall receive eight percent (8%) of Project Revenue collected in each
calendar month.
4.2 In any calendar month in which Project Revenue is collected, the Copywriter shall receive
no less than $250.00.

5.1 Pre-approved expenses are reimbursed at cost against receipts. Expenses are not part of
the fees above.

6. PAYMENT
6.1 The Agency pays freelancers by PayPal in US dollars within fifteen (15) days of the end of
each calendar month.

7. GENERAL
7.1 Each freelancer is an independent contractor. This Agreement is governed by the laws of
the State of New York.
