2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Gross Receipts" means all streaming, download and sync income the Label actually
receives for the Master during an Accounting Period.
2.2 "Accounting Period" means one calendar month.

3. ROYALTY SPLIT
3.1 The Artist shall receive fifty percent (50%) of Gross Receipts.
3.2 The Producer shall receive thirty percent (30%) of Gross Receipts.
3.3 The Guitarist shall receive five percent (5%) of Gross Receipts.
3.4 The Label retains the remaining fifteen percent (15%) of Gross Receipts.
