1.3 The Studio collects all revenue from sales of the "Night Market Cookbook" video course

2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Gross Revenue" means all amounts the Studio actually collects from customers for the
Product during a Revenue Period, before any deduction.
2.2 "Revenue Period" means one calendar month.

3. CONTENT
3.1 The Creator will host and present twelve (12) video lessons. The Editor will edit and
color-grade all lessons. Both will deliver final files by the dates agreed in writing.
3.2 The Studio owns the final course. The Creator keeps the right to show clips in a portfolio.

4. REVENUE SHARE
4.1 The Creator shall receive twenty percent (20%) of Gross Revenue up to $5,000.00 in each
Revenue Period, and thirty percent (30%) of Gross Revenue above $5,000.00 in that Revenue Period.
4.2 The Editor shall receive five percent (5%) of Gross Revenue in each Revenue Period.
4.3 The Studio keeps all Gross Revenue not paid out under this Section 4.
4.4 The Studio may, at its discretion, pay the Creator a performance bonus for exceptional
results. Any bonus must be agreed in a separate written note.

5. REFUND RESERVE
5.1 Ten percent (10%) of each payment due to the Creator shall be held in a refund reserve
and released sixty (60) days after the end of the Revenue Period.
5.2 Refunds and chargebacks during that time may be taken from the reserve.

6. PAYMENT
6.1 The Studio pays each party by PayPal within ten (10) business days after the end of each
Revenue Period, in US dollars.

7. GENERAL
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
